2. Role 1: Public Citizen / Farmer Applicant Manual

Portal URL: http://localhost/alfgb
Supported Languages: English & Urdu (اردو)
Tracking Credentials: Application ID + 13-Digit CNIC (Password)

2.1 Browsing Schemes & Switching Portal Language

  1. Visit the public portal homepage and use the EN | اردو toggle in the header to view the portal in English or Urdu.
  2. Browse ongoing subsidy schemes under Schemes → On Going Schemes or express interest in upcoming Chief Minister initiatives under Proposed For CM.
Figure 2.1 — Public Citizen Portal Homepage with Bilingual (EN / اردو) Toggle & Active Schemes

2.2 Applying Online & Instant CNIC Verification

  1. Click Apply Now on the desired scheme (e.g., Distribution of Wheat Seed — Cost Sharing 50:50).
  2. CNIC Verification: Enter your 13-digit CNIC and click Verify CNIC to auto-populate your personal and geographic details from the verified registry.
  3. Agronomic & Scheme Fields: Enter your District, Tehsil, Union Council, Village, Active Mobile Number (03XXXXXXXXX), Available Land (Kanals), Last Year Cultivated Area, and Demanded Seed Bags.
  4. Submit & Receive SMS: Upon submission, the portal displays your Application Number (e.g. ALF-20261028-00105) and dispatches a bilingual Registration SMS with your Application ID and CNIC as your password.
Figure 2.2 — Citizen Scheme Online Application Form with CNIC Verification & Agronomic Fields

2.3 Tracking Application Status, Downloading Challan & Paying via GBPay PSID

  1. Open Track Application (/track) and enter your Application Number and 13-digit CNIC.
  2. When your application is Approved / Challan Issued:
    • Click Download Challan PDF to print your official 3-copy Bank Challan showing your Approved Bags, 50% Farmer Share Bill, and 20-Digit GBPay PSID.
    • Pay digitally using the 20-digit PSID via any 1Link banking app/ATM (which automatically clears your application and sends a Payment Success SMS), or upload your stamped bank receipt on the tracking page.
Figure 2.3 — Citizen Application Status Tracking, Challan Download & Payment Proof Upload Portal

5. Role 4 & Role 5: Division Director & District Deputy Director (DD) Manual

Roles: Division Director (Divisional Scope) · District Deputy Director / District Officer (District Scope)

5.1 Applications Review Queue & Multi-Criteria Filtering (/admin/applications)

Filter applications by keyword (Application ID, Name, CNIC, Phone, Village), Status/Stage, Department, District, Scheme, Available Land (Min/Max Kanals), and Seed Bags Required, and export filtered lists to Excel, CSV, or PDF.

Figure 5.1 — Applications Review Queue & Multi-Filter Ledger (/admin/applications)

5.2 Desk Review, Field Survey Delegation, Quantity Approval & Challan Issuance (/admin/applications/{id})

  1. Request Correction: Send specific remarks to the citizen (`correction_required`), automatically triggering a bilingual Correction SMS.
  2. Delegate Field Survey: Assign an active District Field Officer with a due date and inspection focus (`field_survey_assigned`).
  3. Approve with Authorized Quantity Override: Select Approved in the Update Status / Transition card and specify the Approved Quantity (e.g., approving 3 bags where 5 were demanded). The system automatically:
    • Validates District Quota availability,
    • Calculates the 50% Farmer Share Challan Bill based on the Approved Quantity and district bag rate,
    • Generates the Challan Number and 20-Digit GBPay PSID, and
    • Sends the bilingual Approval SMS with {approved_bags}, {challan_amount}, {psid}, and {due_date}.
  4. Reject Application: Select Rejected and provide a mandatory rejection reason to dispatch the bilingual Rejection SMS.
Figure 5.2 — Application Review Detail: Demanded vs. Approved Stock, District Quota & 20-Digit GBPay PSID Card

6. Role 6: Field Officer / Site Surveyor Manual

Role: Field Officer
Scope: Applications Assigned for Field Verification within the District
  1. Open Sidebar → Applications to view your assigned field survey queue.
  2. Click View on an assigned application and record:
    • Visit Schedule (visit_scheduled): Log the planned inspection date.
    • Visit Completion (visit_completed): Confirm physical site inspection.
    • Survey Findings, GPS & Photos (findings_submitted): Record verified cultivable land (Kanals), recommended seed bags, at least 3 GPS boundary coordinates, and geo-tagged land inspection photos.
  3. Once submitted, the application automatically transitions to findings_submitted for final approval by the District Deputy Director.

7. Role 7: Accounts Officer & GBPay 1Link Billing Manual

Role: Accounts Officer
Key Screens: /admin/gbpay & Application Challan Verification Card
  • Automated GBPay 1Link Clearance: When a citizen pays their 20-digit PSID via 1Link/GBPay, the system automatically marks the bill as paid, transitions the application to Eligible Beneficiary, and sends the bilingual Payment Success SMS (gbpay_payment_success) with the bill paid amount ({amount_paid}) and PSID.
  • Manual Bank Receipt Verification: For citizens who upload a physical bank deposit slip (`payment_proof_submitted`), inspect the receipt image in the Challan & Payment card and click Verify Payment (Clear Beneficiary).
Figure 7.1 — GBPay Payment Gateway Configuration, 20-Digit PSID Prefix Rules & Bill Ledger (/admin/gbpay)

8. Role 8: Distribution Officer & Stock Logistics Manual

Role: Distribution Officer
Key Screens: /admin/stock, /admin/stock/centers, /admin/stock/receipts, /admin/stock/disbursements
  1. Inventory Management (/admin/stock): Manage Divisional/District Collection Centers, record Stock Receipts (Stock-In), Inter-District Transfers, and Wasted/Damaged stock.
  2. Beneficiary Stock Disbursement (/admin/stock/disbursements):
    • Select an Eligible Beneficiary (verified payment) at the Collection Center counter.
    • Enter the quantity handed over (up to the remaining Approved Quantity), record receiver details, and click Confirm & Disburse Stock.
    • Print the official Disbursement Handover Voucher; the system automatically sends the bilingual Stock Disbursed SMS to the farmer.
